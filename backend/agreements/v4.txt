## 7. Revenue Share

7.1 The Licensee will pay the Licensor thirty-three percent (33%) of the Net Revenue attributable to the Licensor's Licensed Works (the "Revenue Share"), except that the Revenue Share is fifty percent (50%) for Exclusive Buyouts under Section 3.4(f) and for the AI Training Field of Use for Licensed Works under an Exclusive AI Election under Section 3.5, and Subscription Plans are paid as set out in Section 7.6. 7.2 "Net Revenue" means all amounts actually received by the Licensee from Sublicensees for licenses of the Licensed Works, less only: (a) payment processing and currency conversion fees; (b) sales, use, value-added and similar taxes collected or remitted, and amounts withheld for taxes by a Sublicensee or government; and (c) refunds, chargebacks and credits actually issued. No other costs, including the Licensee's overhead, hosting, processing or marketing costs, may be deducted.

7.3 Except for Subscription Plans, where a Sublicense covers works of more than one licensor (for example, a dataset license), Net Revenue from that Sublicense is allocated to each Licensed Work pro rata by its share of the total audio duration of all works delivered under that Sublicense, counting a Licensed Work and its Stems as one work. Where a Sublicense covers a single Licensed Work, all Net Revenue from it is attributable to that work.

7.4 Splits. Where ownership splits for a Licensed Work have been entered on the Platform, the Licensee may pay each co-owner registered on the Platform their share of the Revenue Share as entered, and otherwise shall pay the full Revenue Share to the Licensor. Payment in line with the splits on record discharges the Licensee's payment obligation; the Licensor is responsible for any amounts owed to co-owners not registered on the Platform.

7.5 Performance and mechanical royalties. Nothing in this Agreement affects the Licensor's right to collect, through its performing rights organization, collective management organization, publisher or distributor, the writer's share of public performance royalties or any mechanical royalties arising from uses of the Licensed Works, and the Licensee does not share in those amounts.

7.6 Subscription Artist Pool. Each calendar month, the Licensee forms the "Artist Pool" from the Net Revenue it receives from Subscription Plans, as follows. (a) Sync plans: thirty-three percent (33%) of the Net Revenue from Subscription Plans for the Sync Field of Use is allocated to each Licensed Work pro rata by the number of Registered Projects under those plans that used it during that month. (b) AI training plans: the Net Revenue from Subscription Plans for the AI Training Field of Use is divided by the total number of uses of Licensed Works in AI training Registered Projects during that month (the "Per-Use Amount"). Each use of a Licensed Work earns fifty percent (50%) of the Per-Use Amount if the work was under an Exclusive AI Election when the project was registered, and thirty-three percent (33%) of the Per-Use Amount otherwise. (c) A Licensed Work used in several Registered Projects is counted once for each project, and a Licensed Work downloaded for an AI training project is counted once for that project. The Licensor receives the allocations for its Licensed Works. Subscription discounts, refunds and chargebacks reduce Net Revenue.

7.7 Composition and recording shares. Where ownership splits for a Licensed Work have been entered on the Platform, the Revenue Share attributable to that work is divided equally between the musical composition and the sound recording. Fifty percent (50%) follows the composition splits, of which one half is divided among the writers and one half among the publishers, each according to their shares as entered. The other fifty percent (50%) is divided among the master owners according to their shares as entered. Where the Licensor owns 100% of a Licensed Work, the full Revenue Share is paid to the Licensor.
